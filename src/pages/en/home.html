Typical questions

What ITCoreNet takes on

Many IT topics work “somehow” – until they cost time, money or data. Typical questions ITCoreNet helps with:

Day-to-day IT & infrastructure

Slow systems

Employees wait for logins, applications or files. What is the cause – and what does the waiting cost?

New workstations take too long

It takes days before a new computer is ready for use. How can this become predictable, even across several locations?

Backups without certainty

Data is backed up – but can it actually be restored in an emergency?

Unclear data storage

Where is the company's data actually stored – and who has access to it?

Ageing hardware

Computers, servers and network devices age. When is replacement worthwhile – and how can it be done without downtime?

Major changes

A migration, a move or a new location is coming up. Who plans, coordinates and implements it?

Microsoft environment, cloud & AI

Active Directory and Entra ID

On-premises AD, Entra ID or both in a hybrid setup: how do users, groups and sign-ins stay consistent and secure?

Exchange and Exchange Online

Migrating mailboxes, setting up a hybrid configuration or retiring your own Exchange server – how can this be done without disrupting daily work?

Microsoft 365 under control

Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive are licensed – but are they properly configured, secured and organised?

Managing devices centrally

How can laptops and smartphones be set up automatically with Intune, kept up to date and locked if lost?

Keeping licences in check

Which Microsoft licences are really needed – and where are you paying twice or for unused ones?

Using AI sensibly

Copilot and other AI tools: where do they add real value – and which data may go into them?

IT security

IT security with priorities

Multi-factor sign-in, updates, permissions and staff awareness: where does the business stand – and what comes first?

E-mail security

Spam, phishing and malware still reach the inbox? Filtering, encryption and archiving – for example with Hornetsecurity.

Firewall and secure remote access

Is the firewall up to date and properly configured, and can employees reach the company network securely via VPN – for example with Sophos firewalls?
