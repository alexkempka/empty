Typical questions

What ITCoreNet takes on

Many IT topics work “somehow” – until they cost time, money or data. Typical questions ITCoreNet helps with:

Slow systems

Employees wait for logins, applications or files. What is the cause – and what does the waiting cost?

New workstations take too long

It takes days before a new computer is ready for use. How can this become predictable, even across several locations?

Backups without certainty

Data is backed up – but can it actually be restored in an emergency?

Secure remote access

How can employees work securely and conveniently on the road or from home?

Unclear data storage

Where is the company's data actually stored – and who has access to it?

Major changes

A migration, a move or a new location is coming up. Who plans, coordinates and implements it?
