About ITCoreNet

ITCoreNet is an owner-managed IT consultancy run by Alessandro Kempka, based in Rödermark, Germany.

Consulting that gets implemented

The focus is on combining IT consulting with hands-on implementation. Concepts do not stay on paper: whoever plans the solution also supports its implementation – and, if required, its day-to-day operation.
