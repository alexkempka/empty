# About ITCoreNet

ITCoreNet is an owner-managed IT consultancy run by Alessandro Kempka, based in Rödermark, Germany.

## Consulting that gets implemented

The focus is on combining IT consulting with hands-on implementation. Concepts do not stay on
paper: whoever plans the solution also supports its implementation – and, if required, its
day-to-day operation.

## Experience

Alessandro Kempka has many years of experience in international IT projects, including
infrastructure, migrations, hardware standardisation and IT management.
[View selected project experience](/en/project-experience/)

## Way of working

- Clear agreements on goals, scope and effort
- Transparent decision support instead of jargon
- Concept and implementation from a single source
- Projects in German and English
