# Services

ITCoreNet covers the path from the first assessment to day-to-day operations. You can commission a
single area or combine several.

## IT Consulting & IT Management {#consulting}

Good IT decisions require a clear view of the current state, goals and costs. ITCoreNet takes on
the tasks of an IT lead – on a one-off basis, for a project or as ongoing support (CIO as a
Service / vCIO).

- Assessment and evaluation of the existing IT
- IT concepts and roadmaps, including network and security concepts
- Budget planning and decision support
- IT policies, processes and procedures (IT service management)
- Project management, including international projects

## IT Projects & Infrastructure {#projects}

A concept only pays off once it has been implemented. ITCoreNet plans IT projects and implements
them hands-on.

- Migrations and upgrades, for example of directory services and e-mail systems
- IT integration during mergers and acquisitions
- IT relocations and setting up new locations
- Servers, networks and virtualisation
- Backup and recovery
- Introduction of cloud services
- Rollouts of new hardware and software

## Hardware Procurement & Lifecycle {#hardware}

Standardised devices reduce the effort for setup, support and replacement. ITCoreNet helps with the
selection, procurement and management of your hardware.

- Standards for workstations and print solutions
- Procurement of hardware and software
- Provisioning and rollout
- Support throughout the lifecycle up to planned replacement

## Managed Services {#managed-services}

Once the project is finished, operations begin. If required, ITCoreNet provides ongoing support for
your IT. The tasks and scope are agreed individually – to match the size and requirements of your
IT.
