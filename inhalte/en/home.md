# Advise. Implement. Operate.

ITCoreNet supports businesses with IT consulting, IT management and hands-on implementation – from
concepts and project management to infrastructure, rollouts and hardware procurement, through to
managed services for day-to-day operations. Consulting and implementation from a single source.

## Who ITCoreNet works for

ITCoreNet works for businesses that want to develop their IT in a targeted way – for example
because there is no in-house IT lead, a major project is coming up, or there is not enough internal
capacity to carry it out.

Private customers are also welcome to get in touch. Scope and services are then agreed
individually.

## What ITCoreNet takes on

Many IT topics work "somehow" – until they cost time, money or data. Typical questions ITCoreNet
helps with:

### Slow systems
Employees wait for logins, applications or files. What is the cause – and what does the waiting
cost?

### New workstations take too long
It takes days before a new computer is ready for use. How can this become predictable, even across
several locations?

### Backups without certainty
Data is backed up – but can it actually be restored in an emergency?

### Secure remote access
How can employees work securely and conveniently on the road or from home?

### Unclear data storage
Where is the company's data actually stored – and who has access to it?

### Major changes
A migration, a move or a new location is coming up. Who plans, coordinates and implements it?

## How ITCoreNet works

1. **Analysis** – assessment of the IT and the requirements
2. **Concept** – solution, effort and priorities as a clear basis for decisions
3. **Implementation** – project management and hands-on implementation
4. **Operation** – ongoing support as a managed service, if required

You can start at any stage – even if only a single step is needed.
