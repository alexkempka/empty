# Selected Project Experience

The following projects come from the owner's professional experience in previous employment.
**They are not ITCoreNet client projects.** All details are anonymised; figures are rounded.

## Consolidation of an international directory and e-mail infrastructure

**Task:** Merging several directory domains into one new target domain, with locations on several
continents, and migrating the e-mail platform to the then-current version.

**Scope:**
- More than 2,000 mailboxes
- Server standardisation
- High availability for authentication and e-mail services

## IT integration following a merger

**Task:** Requirements analysis (current and target state) and transfer of the central services
into the new organisational structure.

**Scope:** Including directory service, e-mail, ERP system and collaboration platform.

## Global standardisation of client hardware

**Task:** Introduction of a single global hardware standard for all client computers.

**Scope:**
- Central installation process for all devices
- Global and country-specific invoicing
- Global shipping and delivery

## Redesign of a site network across Europe and North America

**Task:** New design of the wide area network (WAN) for several dozen locations.

**Scope:**
- Failover data centres
- Consolidation of the headquarters data centre and relocation to an external high-security data
  centre

## Introduction of mobile device management

**Task:** Setup and configuration of a mobile device management (MDM) platform.

**Scope:** Several thousand mobile devices – iOS and Android smartphones as well as tablets.
